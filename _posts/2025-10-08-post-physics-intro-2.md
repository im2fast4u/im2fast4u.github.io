Most people think magic is fake. But when I started reading the book *Six Easy Pieces*, I realized that physics is like magic - except it's true. The things I learned in just two chapters already feel like they belong in a science fiction movie.

## The Magic of the Tiny World
I found out that everything is made up of atoms. But atoms aren't like little matbles - they're mostly empty space. That means if you shring down small enough, you could go right through things. It sounds impossible, but it's true. And when I got to the part of quantum mechanics, my brain exploded (not literally). I read that tiny particles can be in many places at once. Not "here or there," but "here and there." Also, you can't even know exactly how fast they're going and where they are at the same time.
