Have you ever heard that you can't take the square root of a negative number? In some equations, there are situations where you must take the square root of a negative number in order to solve it. The square root of -1 ($\sqrt{-1}$) is known as $i$, the imaginary unit. Complex numbers are terms that have the imaginary unit $i$ such as the term $3i$.
