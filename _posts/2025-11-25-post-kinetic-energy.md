## What Is Kinetic Energy?
Kinetic energy is the energy of motion. An example of using kinetic energy is when a car is driving, or when you are running around. Like in the other post, gravitational potential energy is the energy in an object based on the position in a gravitational field and on the height and weight of the object. So, if a car stops on a red light and drives when it turns green, the car's potential energy turns into kinetic energy.

## How the Formula Works
The formula for kinetic energy is $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$, where $m$ is the mass and $v$ is the velocity.
